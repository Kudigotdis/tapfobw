About TapFo (Page)

TapFo - Everything. Connecting Communities, Empowering Local Businesses.
TapFo is Botswana’s essential, offline-first digital directory and advertising platform. We are dedicated to bridging the digital divide by making reliable local business information, job opportunities, and services accessible to everyone, regardless of constant internet connectivity.

Our Mission: Local Focus, Global Standards
In a world demanding constant data, TapFo ensures you can always find the services you need in your Town and Area. We provide verified, up-to-date listings for businesses ranging from large enterprises to micro-enterprises (Hustler Visibility), ensuring the local economy is always discoverable.

The Power of the Human Touch: Our Agent Network
Unlike pure digital solutions, our success is built on trust and community. TapFo is powered by a dedicated network of local Agents—tech-savvy youth who are the face of our service.

Verification: Our Agents personally verify user and business identities, ensuring data accuracy and safety. This allows VIP users to earn the sought-after Agent-validated "Verified ID" badge, boosting their trust with local employers.
Support: Agents ensure new businesses are correctly listed in the TapFo DataBase and provide face-to-face support and activation for our premium services. //

Powered by 86th Avenue
TapFo is a flagship product developed and managed by 86th Avenue, an agency committed to sustainable local economic and professional growth. We invest directly in our community:

Staff Investment: We provide our Agents with robust support, including Advanced Technical Training (Google Sheets, Digital Marketing) and a comprehensive benefits package designed for Financial Security, Health, and Lifestyle Support.

Commitment: 86th Avenue ensures TapFo operates ethically, fostering a supportive community culture through mandatory training meetings and peer-learning initiatives.

Our goal is simple: To provide everything the local consumer needs, delivered by a team they can trust.
